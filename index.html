How to play
  1. Take turns tapping a cell to drop an orb in it. Empty cells and your own are fair game.
  2. A cell bursts when it holds as many orbs as it has neighbours: 2 in a corner, 3 on an edge, 4 in the middle. Orbs start to shake when a cell is one away.
  3. A burst throws one orb into each neighbour and turns those cells your colour, orbs and all.
  4. Bursts set off more bursts. Knock your opponent's last orb off the board to win.
