How to play
  1. Take turns tapping the gap between two neighbouring dots to draw a line.
  2. Draw the fourth side of a box and it's yours. Your stamp goes in it and you go again.
  3. Close two boxes with one line and both are yours. Keep going as long as you keep closing boxes.
  4. Careful with third sides: they hand the next player a box. When every line is drawn, most boxes wins.
